MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on March 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date") between Contoso Labs Inc. and Initech Ltd, each a "party" and together the "parties".

1. Purpose. The parties wish to exchange confidential information to evaluate a possible business relationship.

2. Obligations. Each party shall hold the other party's confidential information in confidence, use it only for the stated purpose, and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

3. Term. This Agreement lasts for 2 years from the Effective Date. The confidentiality obligations continue for 3 years after it ends.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by written notice.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
