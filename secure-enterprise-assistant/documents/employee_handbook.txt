Employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

Remote work is permitted up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees must be reachable during core hours of 10am to 3pm.

To reset a forgotten password, use the self-service portal at the IT help page. The IT help desk never asks for your password by phone or email.

Expense claims must be filed within 30 days with receipts attached. Claims over 500 dollars need manager approval.
