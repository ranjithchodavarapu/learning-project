title: Q3 Financial Forecast
classification: confidential
department: finance
---
The Q3 revenue forecast is 48.2 million dollars, a 12 percent increase over Q2.

Operating margin is projected at 18 percent. The forecast assumes the enterprise renewal of Northwind completes in August.

The finance team plans a 3.1 million dollar reserve for the pending acquisition of Contoso Labs. This information is confidential until the announcement.
