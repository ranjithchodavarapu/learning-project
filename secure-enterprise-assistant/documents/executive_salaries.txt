title: Executive Compensation 2024
classification: restricted
department: hr
---
The chief executive officer base salary is 640000 dollars with a target bonus of 50 percent.

The chief financial officer base salary is 410000 dollars with a target bonus of 40 percent.

The chief technology officer base salary is 395000 dollars with a target bonus of 40 percent.

Executive severance terms are set at twelve months of base salary and must not be disclosed outside the compensation committee.
