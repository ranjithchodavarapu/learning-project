title: Engineering On-Call Runbook
classification: internal
department: engineering
---
The on-call engineer rotates weekly every Monday at 10am. The on-call phone is handed over in person or via the team channel.

For a production outage, page the incident commander first, then open an incident channel named after the date and service.

Deployments are frozen every Friday after 2pm. Emergency fixes need approval from the engineering director.

Database failover is automatic. Manual failover requires two engineers to confirm the runbook step before executing.
